## Sales Performance Analysis

## Overview
Analysis of global sales data using Python, Pandas and Power BI.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- JupyterLab
- Power BI

## Questions Answered
- Which month had the highest sales?
- Which country made the most revenue?
- Which product line sold the most?
- Who are the top 10 customers?
- Which deal size brings most revenue?

## Key Findings
- Highest sales month: sales peacked towards the end of the year likely driven by holiday promotion and end-of-year demand.
- Top revenue country: The United State generated the most revenue, accounting for 35% of global sales.
- Best-selling product line: Electronics like Classic cars were the strongest product line,showing consistent growth across all regions.
- Top 10 customers : The largest customers contributed disproportionately to the revenue with the top 1 (Euro Shopping Channel) making 30.87% of the total sale.
- Deal size impact: Large deal size brought in the heighest revenue, but medium-sized deal were more frequent and stable.
