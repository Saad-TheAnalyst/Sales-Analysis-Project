# Sales Performance Analysis Dashboard

## Overview
A comprehensive analysis of global sales data using 
Python, Pandas, Matplotlib and Power BI to uncover 
revenue trends, product performance, customer patterns 
and order insights across multiple countries and years.

## Tools Used
- Python
- Pandas
- Matplotlib
- Power BI
- JupyterLab

## Questions Answered
- Which country generates the most revenue?
- Which product line is most profitable?
- Which month consistently has the highest sales?
- Who are the top 10 customers by revenue?
- Which cities generate the most revenue?
- How did sales grow year over year?
- What is the order status breakdown?
- Which deal size generates the most revenue?
- How are customers segmented by revenue?

## Key Findings

### Overall Performance
- Total revenue across all years was $8,286,886.
- 2004 was the best performing year with $3,913,700 
  in revenue — a 35% increase over 2003.
- 2005 data is incomplete showing only 5 months 
  of data.

### Monthly Trends
- November is consistently the highest revenue month 
  across all years driven by holiday season demand.
- October and November together account for the 
  majority of annual revenue showing strong 
  seasonality in sales.

### Product Line Performance
- Classic Cars is the best selling product line 
  with $2,968,546 in total revenue.
- Trucks and Buses has the highest revenue per 
  unit at $87.91 making it the most profitable 
  per sale.
- Trains is the lowest performing product line 
  with only $203,804 in revenue.

### Customer Analysis
- 92 unique customers were identified.
- 70 customers fall in the Medium segment 
  spending between $50,000 and $150,000.
- Only 5 customers are in the Large segment 
  spending over $150,000.
- 17 customers are Small spending under $50,000.

### City Performance
- Madrid is the top city by revenue with $902,094 
  followed by San Rafael with $530,587.
- New York City comes third with $450,418.

### Order Status
- 92.7% of all orders were successfully shipped 
  showing strong fulfillment performance.
- Only 60 orders were cancelled representing 
  2.1% of total orders.

### Deal Size Analysis
- Medium deals generated the most revenue at 
  $4,961,736 across 1,384 orders.
- Large deals have the highest average order 
  value at $4,705 per order.
- Small deals are the most frequent but generate 
  the least revenue per order at $2,020.
